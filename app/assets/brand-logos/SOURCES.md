# Brand logo sources

These marks are used only to identify brands in the personal rating leaderboard. Trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

The local icons for Alani Nu, GHOST, Gorilla Mind, RAZE, Reign, and RYSE were retrieved from the favicon associated with each brand's official website on 2026-10-30. The C4 Energy mark was retrieved from an official Nutrabolt press-release asset hosted by PR Newswire.

The following leaderboard marks use scalable SVG artwork:

- CELSIUS: https://companieslogo.com/celsius-holdings/logo/
- Monster Energy: https://worldvectorlogo.com/logo/monster-energy-1
- Red Bull: https://www.redbull.com/v3/resources/images/client/header/redbullcom-logo_double-with-text.svg
- Rockstar Energy: https://www.rockstarenergy.com/cdn/shop/files/2024-RS-Combo-Logo-Primary.svg

Each SVG was visually checked against the brand and scanned for scripts, event handlers, external references, and `foreignObject` content before being added locally.

The application does not hotlink these assets. New brands safely fall back to generated initials until a curated local mark is added.
